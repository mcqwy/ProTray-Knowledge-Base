# 海外网红（KOL）筛选与邀约指南

> **适用产品**：消费品、园艺用品、家居用品等适合视觉展示的产品。

## 🔍 网红筛选标准矩阵

| 评估维度 | 考察点 | 合格标准 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **粉丝画像** | 国家、年龄、兴趣标签 | 70%以上粉丝在目标市场 |
| **内容调性** | 视频风格是否专业、真实 | 与品牌定位一致 |
| **粉丝互动率** | 点赞/评论/播放量 | 互动率 ≥ 3%（剔除僵尸粉） |
| **是否有带货经验** | 过往合作案例 | 至少有3次成功带货记录 |
| **预算匹配** | 报价范围 | 在预算内或愿意接受佣金模式 |

## ✉️ 网红邀约邮件模板

**主题：** Collaboration Invitation - [Product Name] Free Sample + Commission

```
Hi [Influencer Name],

We've been following your content on [Platform] and really enjoy your videos about [specific topic].

We are a [Country]-based manufacturer of [Product]. We'd love to invite you to try our products. Here's how we work:
- We send you free samples
- You create an honest review video
- You earn [XX]% commission on every sale made through your unique link

If you're interested, we can send samples first. No pressure at all — you can decide after checking the quality.

Looking forward to your reply!

Best,
[Your Name]
[Company Name]
[WhatsApp]
```
