## 🔍 精准搜索指令（布尔搜索）

在LinkedIn搜索框输入以下指令组合：

**示例1：找美国园艺用品采购经理**
```
(title: "Purchasing" OR title: "Sourcing" OR title: "Buyer") AND ("Gardening" OR "Horticulture") AND (company: "USA" OR location: "United States")
```

**示例2：找欧洲育苗盘经销商**
```
(title: "Sales Manager" OR title: "Director") AND ("Greenhouse" OR "Nursery") AND NOT ("Recruiter" OR "HR")
```
