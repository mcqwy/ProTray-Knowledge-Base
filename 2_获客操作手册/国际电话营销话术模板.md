# 国际电话营销话术模板

> **适用场景**：通过海关数据、黄页获取电话后，直接致电目标客户。

## 📞 开场白（30秒内吸引注意力）

**话术模板**：
```
Hello, this is [Your Name] from [Company Name]. We are a [Country]-based manufacturer of [Product]. I noticed your company is in the [Industry] business, and I think our products might fit your sourcing needs. Do you have 2 minutes for a quick introduction?
```

## 🎯 应对常见拒绝

| 客户回应 | 应对话术 |
| :--- | :--- |
| "I'm busy right now" | I totally understand. I'll send you a brief email with more details. May I have your email address? |
| "We already have a supplier" | That's great! It shows you value quality. Some of our current clients switched from other suppliers and saved 15% on costs. Would you be open to comparing a sample? |
| "Just send me an email" | Will do right away. May I ask your name and get your WhatsApp for quick follow-ups? |

## 📝 通话结束后必做
1. 立即记录关键信息（客户需求、痛点、决策人身份）
2. 当天发送跟进邮件或WhatsApp消息
3. 在CRM中标记跟进状态和下次联系时间
