# 外贸开发信模板库（可直接复制使用）

> **适用场景**：海关数据、LinkedIn获取邮箱后的一对一开发。  
> **核心原则**：简短、痛点前置、突出差异化。

## 📧 模板1：首次接触（针对经销商/批发商）
**主题**：Re: [Product Name] for [Target Market] - Factory Direct

```
Hi [Name],

I noticed [Company Name] specializes in [specific field].

We are a [Country] factory with [X] years of experience in [Product]. Unlike trading companies, we offer:
- **70g PET material** (stronger than standard 50g)
- **Custom sizes & logos** (MOQ: 500pcs)
- **Sample available** in 7 days

Do you currently work with any direct manufacturers? If so, I'd be happy to send you our catalogue and a free sample for comparison.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Title]
[WhatsApp/Phone]
```

## 📧 模板2：跟进展会/海关数据线索
**主题**：Follow-up from [Exhibition Name] / [Data Source]

```
Hi [Name],

We met at [Exhibition] last week / I noticed your company imports [Product] from China.

Just a quick follow-up: we've just upgraded our production line, reducing defect rate to under 0.5% while keeping the same price.

Attached is our latest catalog. If you have any ongoing sourcing needs, I'd love to get on a quick call.

Cheers,
[Your Name]
```

## 📧 模板3：针对高潜力大客户（提供定制方案）
**主题**：Custom [Product] Solution for [Company Name]'s Next Project

```
Dear [Name],

I've studied your website and see you have a strong focus on [specific area].

We specialize in providing **OEM/ODM** solutions. For our clients, we usually:
1. Provide 3D drawings within 48 hours.
2. Offer strict quality inspection reports per batch.
3. Support 30-day return policy.

We have a new design that might fit your upcoming [Q4/Q1] project. Can I share a few design sketches with you?

Regards,
[Your Name]
```
