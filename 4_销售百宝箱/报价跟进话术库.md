# 报价跟进话术库（可直接复制使用）

> **用途**：报价发出后，业务员在不同时间节点使用的跟进话术参考
> **更新日期**：2026-09-05

## 📋 跟进时间线与话术索引

| 时间节点 | 话术编号 | 目的 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Day +3 | H-001 | 确认收到 + 初步询问 |
| Day +7 | H-002 | 补充价值信息 |
| Day +14 | H-003 | 锁定卡点 + 竞品试探 |
| Day +21 | H-004 | 归档前最后尝试 |


## 📧 H-001：首次跟进（报价后 3 天）

**场景**：确认客户是否收到报价，是否有初步疑问。

```
Subject: Quick check on quotation QT-[编号]

Hi [客户名],

Hope this email finds you well. Just a quick follow-up to confirm you received our quotation QT-[编号] sent on [发送日期].

Do you have any initial questions about the specs, pricing, or delivery timeline? Happy to clarify anything.

Looking forward to your feedback.

Best regards,
[您的姓名]
[您的职位]
[您的电话/WhatsApp]
```


## 📧 H-002：二次跟进（报价后 7 天）

**场景**：补充额外价值信息，制造紧迫感（适用于没有回复的客户）。

```
Subject: Update on production schedule / QT-[编号]

Hi [客户名],

Quick update: our production line is currently running at near full capacity for the next 2 months. If you have any upcoming projects, I'd recommend confirming the order by [日期] to secure your production slot.

We've also just received positive feedback from [行业/区域] clients on the 70g rPET material — they mentioned it significantly reduced breakage during transplanting.

Let me know if you'd like to schedule a quick call to discuss.

Best regards,
[您的姓名]
[您的职位]
[您的电话/WhatsApp]
```


## 📧 H-003：三次跟进（报价后 14 天）

**场景**：客户仍不回复，询问是否已对比竞品，主动提出调整方案。

```
Subject: Any adjustments needed? / QT-[编号]

Hi [客户名],

I'm following up to check if you've had a chance to compare our offer with other suppliers. If there's any gap in pricing, specs, or delivery terms, I'd be happy to see if we can adjust to better fit your needs.

We're flexible on:
- MOQ for trial orders
- Custom packaging options
- Payment terms for long-term cooperation

Would a brief 5-minute call help clarify anything?

Looking forward to your reply.

Best regards,
[您的姓名]
[您的职位]
[您的电话/WhatsApp]
```


## 📧 H-004：归档前最后尝试（报价后 21 天）

**场景**：最后一次礼貌跟进，确认客户状态，合理退出或转入长期培育池。

```
Subject: Closing out QT-[编号]

Hi [客户名],

I understand you've been busy — just one final check before I close out quotation QT-[编号].

If this project is still on your radar, I'd be happy to provide updated pricing or samples. If it's been postponed or awarded to another supplier, I totally understand — and I'll keep our file open in case you need us in the future.

Wishing you all the best. Hope we can work together down the road.

Best regards,
[您的姓名]
[您的职位]
[您的电话/WhatsApp]
```


## 💬 WhatsApp/短信快速跟进

**场景**：客户加了 WhatsApp 但邮件不回复，用短消息轻量触达：

```
Hi [客户名], just checked in on email — did you get our quote QT-[编号]? Any initial thoughts? Feel free to reply here if easier. - [您的姓名]
```

```
Hi [客户名], quick one — our production slots are filling up for next quarter. If you have any upcoming orders, let me know and I'll hold a slot for you. No obligation. - [您的姓名]
```
