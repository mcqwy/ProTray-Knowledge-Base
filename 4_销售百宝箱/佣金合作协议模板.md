# 厂代佣金协议模板（可直接复制使用）

> **适用场景**：与海外厂代（独立销售代表）建立佣金合作关系时使用
> **文件类型**：合同模板（复制到Word → 导出PDF → 电子签名）
> **更新日期**：2026-09-05


## 📌 使用方法

### 签署步骤

**步骤 A：准备合同**
将下方的合同内容复制到 Word 文档中，将以下字段替换为您的实际信息：

| 占位符 | 替换为 |
| :--- | :--- |
| `[填写当前日期]` | 签署当天的日期，如：October 15, 2026 |
| `[你的网站英文名/公司名]` | 您的公司英文名称，如：ProTray Supply |
| `[填写厂代负责的区域]` | 该厂代负责的销售区域，如：North America / Europe |
| `[10% to 12%]` | 双方商定的佣金比例，如：10% |
| `[您的国家/地区]` | 管辖法律适用的国家，如：China |
| `[您的姓名]` | 您的全名（拼音） |
| `[您的职位]` | 您的职位，如：Global Sales Director |
| `[您的邮箱]` | 您的商务邮箱 |
| `[填写厂代的英文全名/公司名]` | 厂代的姓名或公司名 |
| `[厂代公司名]` | 厂代的公司名称 |
| `[厂代邮箱]` | 厂代的邮箱 |

**步骤 B：发送给厂代**
当厂代通过 WhatsApp / LinkedIn 与您沟通，达成初步合作意向后：
1. 将 Word 文档另存为 PDF 格式
2. 发送给厂代，附言：
   > *"This is our official commission agreement. Please review it and let me know if you have any questions. If everything looks good, we can sign it electronically."*

**步骤 C：签署方法（推荐方案1）**

| 方案 | 工具 | 操作说明 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **方案1（推荐）** | DocuSign / HelloSign（免费国际电子签名平台） | 将PDF上传至平台 → 输入厂代邮箱 → 系统自动发送签署链接 → 双方在线签名 → 自动生成法律效力，双方各存一份 |
| **方案2（最简单）** | Adobe Reader（"填写和签名"功能） | 厂代在PDF上签名 → 邮件发回 → 您签名确认 → 完成签署 |


## 📄 协议完整模板

> 将下方内的内容全选复制即可

```
COMMISSION AND SALES REPRESENTATION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into as of [填写当前日期, 如: October 15, 2026], by and between:

PRINCIPAL: [你的网站英文名/公司名], hereinafter referred to as "the Principal".

REPRESENTATIVE: [填写厂代的英文全名/公司名], hereinafter referred to as "the Representative".


1. SCOPE OF REPRESENTATION

The Representative is authorized to act as an independent, non-exclusive sales representative to promote and solicit orders for the Principal's seedling trays within the designated territory of [填写厂代负责的区域, 如: North America / United States].


2. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR STATUS

The Representative operates strictly as an independent contractor. This Agreement does not create an employer-employee relationship, partnership, or joint venture. The Representative is solely responsible for their own business expenses, taxes, and insurance.


3. COMMISSION STRUCTURE AND BASIS

3.1 The Principal agrees to pay the Representative a flat commission rate of [10% to 12%] on the GROSS SALES VALUE of each successful order directly procured by the Representative.

3.2 "Gross Sales Value" shall be calculated strictly on the net product value (EXW/FOB basis). For the avoidance of doubt, no commission shall be paid on any shipping fees, ocean freight charges, inland logistics costs, customs duties, or insurance fees added to the buyer's invoice.


4. GUARANTEED PAYMENT TIMELINE

The Principal guarantees an ultra-fast payout policy. Commission for each successful transaction shall be processed and wired to the Representative's designated corporate bank account within five (5) business days after the Principal receives the final/full clear payment from the Buyer.


5. SAMPLE AND MARKETING SUPPORT

The Principal agrees to provide free production samples and marketing catalogs airmailed directly to the Representative's qualified potential accounts. However, each sample request must be pre-approved by the Principal. The Representative must provide verifiable customer information (including but not limited to the client's website, nursery/farm size, and estimated annual purchase volume) prior to shipment.


6. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of one (1) year from the date of signing and shall automatically renew unless terminated. Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by providing thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party via email.


7. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [您的国家/地区].


IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement as of the date first written above.

THE PRINCIPAL:                              THE REPRESENTATIVE:

[您的公司名]                                [厂代公司名]

By: ________________________                By: ________________________

Name: [您的姓名]                            Name: ______________________

Title: [您的职位]                           Title: _____________________

Email: [您的邮箱]                            Email: ____________________
```


## ⚠️ 使用提醒

| 注意事项 | 说明 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **佣金比例** | 10%-12% 是建议区间，请根据产品利润和厂代能力灵活调整 |
| **区域条款** | 如需独家代理权，可将"non-exclusive"改为"exclusive"，并在第1条中明确独家区域边界 |
| **样品支持** | 建议每次寄样前保留厂代提供的客户信息邮件记录，便于后续追溯 |
| **合同份数** | 双方各持一份签署完毕的PDF正本，具有同等法律效力 |
| **法律效力** | 使用DocuSign/HelloSign签署的电子签名在欧美具有完全法律效力 |
| **管辖法律** | 建议咨询法务确认管辖法律条款是否符合您的实际业务需求 |
