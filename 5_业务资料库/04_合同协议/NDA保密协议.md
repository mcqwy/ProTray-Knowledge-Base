### 1. PURPOSE

This Agreement is entered into for the purpose of discussing a potential business cooperation between the Parties. During such discussions, each Party may disclose to the other certain proprietary and confidential information.


### 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" shall include all information disclosed by either Party to the other, whether orally or in writing, including but not limited to:

- Product specifications, designs, and technical data
- Customer lists and supplier information
- Pricing and financial information
- Business plans and marketing strategies
- Manufacturing processes and trade secrets


### 3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

3.1 The Receiving Party shall:

- (a) Use the Confidential Information **solely** for the Purpose described in Section 1
- (b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent
- (c) Limit internal access to personnel with a "need to know"
- (d) Not copy or reproduce Confidential Information except as reasonably required

3.2 The Receiving Party shall protect Confidential Information using the **same degree of care** it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.


### 4. EXCLUSIONS

Confidential Information does **not** include information that:

- Is or becomes publicly known through no fault of the Receiving Party
- Was in the Receiving Party's possession prior to disclosure
- Is independently developed by the Receiving Party
- Is required to be disclosed by law or court order


### 5. TERM

5.1 This Agreement shall remain in effect for a period of **two (2) years** from the Effective Date.

5.2 The confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this Agreement and continue for a period of **five (5) years** from the date of disclosure.


### 6. RETURN OF MATERIALS

Upon written request, the Receiving Party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify such destruction in writing.


### 7. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [Your Country/Region].


### 8. MISCELLANEOUS

8.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

8.2 No failure or delay in exercising any right shall operate as a waiver.

8.3 This Agreement may not be assigned without prior written consent.
